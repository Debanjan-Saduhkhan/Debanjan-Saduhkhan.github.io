Research Overview

Why Not Conventional Clustering?

Conventional clustering fails to capture the complex, high-dimensional relationships in behavioral data, especially when modalities are mixed (e.g., text, images, graphs).

Why Behavioral Graphs → Images?

Transforming behavioral graphs into images enables the use of powerful computer vision and deep learning techniques for clustering and analysis.

RAG Methodology

Combining retrieval-augmented generation with multimodal data fusion to improve clustering, retrieval, and interpretability of user behavior.
