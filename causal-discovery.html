EFfECT-RL: Enabling Framework for Establishing Causality and Triggering Engagement through RL

CIKM 2024 D. Sadhukhan et al.

Abstract

This paper introduces a novel causal discovery technique for volatile user gameplay behavior, combining causal inference with reinforcement learning to create interpretable engagement strategies. The framework addresses the challenge of establishing causality in dynamic user behavior patterns.
