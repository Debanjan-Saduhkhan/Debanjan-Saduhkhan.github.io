EFfECT-RL: Enabling Framework for Establishing Causality and Triggering Engagement through RL

CIKM 2024 D. Sadhukhan et al.

Abstract

This paper introduces EFfECT-RL, a novel framework that combines causal discovery with reinforcement learning to enhance user engagement in gaming platforms. The framework addresses the challenge of understanding causal relationships in user behavior while optimizing engagement strategies through RL.

Key Contributions

  • Novel integration of causal discovery with reinforcement learning
  • Interpretable engagement optimization framework
  • Real-world validation on gaming platform data
  • Significant improvement in user engagement metrics

t-RELOAD: A Reinforcement Learning-based Recommendation for Outcome-driven Application

AI-ML Systems 2023 D. Sadhukhan et al.

Abstract

t-RELOAD presents an innovative approach to recommendation systems using reinforcement learning, specifically designed for outcome-driven applications. The system learns optimal recommendation strategies through continuous interaction with users.

Key Features

  • Temporal-aware recommendation learning
  • Outcome-driven optimization

  • Performance evaluation on real datasets

Deep RL-based User-Ranking for Campaign Design

Games24x7 Internal D. Sadhukhan et al.

Abstract

An interpretable deep reinforcement learning approach for user ranking that helps design effective campaigns. The system learns optimal ranking strategies while maintaining interpretability for business stakeholders.
